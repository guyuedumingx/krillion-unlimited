## Where to earn relevant links

Recommended order is an editorial choice based on audience fit and effort, not a measured SEO authority score. The public GitHub repository and homepage link already exist; these are not new links earned in V10. Nothing was posted to external communities during this request.

1. **itch.io project page — first new listing.** A free public game page with a short gameplay clip, screenshots, seven-language support, and a clear Play on the website link. Use relevant trivia/educational/browser-game tags if actually available in its editor. The platform's creator documentation permits external links and formatted project descriptions. Prefer linking to the canonical homepage; a geography development log can link to the Geography explanation. Do not iframe the deployed site: its CSP disallows framing; hosting an HTML5 build would be a separate packaging task.
   Source: https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started ; HTML5 packaging: https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5 .
2. **GitHub — existing public project.** Keep the About homepage and README entry. Explain how theme selection, deterministic sharing, and localization work. A relevant developer who cites the project can create an editorial mention; stars alone are not backlinks or search-index acceptance. Repository: https://github.com/guyuedumingx/krillion-unlimited .
3. **X — gameplay and questions.** Use the prepared bilingual launch posts plus an actual gameplay clip or one sample question. Link to the homepage for general launches and the relevant canonical guide for theme posts. Treat social referrals as distribution and feedback; no search-ranking benefit is promised. Publish from the user's own account only when separately instructed.
4. **Geography/education creators and trivia blogs — relevant editorial links.** Pitch the Geography guide as a free recall exercise with clearly defined limits and cited learning sources. Sports trivia publishers get the Sports guide. Ask whether it fits an existing resource list instead of buying keyword-anchor links. Outreach recipients and contact details are not researched or verified yet; no emails were sent.
5. **Product Hunt — later product launch.** Prepare a real playable demo, screenshots, maker explanation, and homepage link after collecting early feedback. It supports maker launches, but its broad product audience is less specifically matched to trivia players than itch.io. Source: https://www.producthunt.com/launch .
