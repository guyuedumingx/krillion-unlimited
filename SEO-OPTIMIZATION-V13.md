# Search intent optimization — 2026-10-13

The user supplied Search Console observations: `krillion en español` had 5 clicks and 14.7% CTR; `krillion sports` had 156 impressions and 1 click (about 0.64% CTR). German, Portuguese, Geography Unlimited and Unblocked queries were also reported. Reporting dates, country/device breakdowns and average positions were not provided; these numbers were not independently retrieved. They establish query activity, not low competition or a measured Ahrefs KD.

## Measurement after launch

Compare seven-day periods in Search Console, using the exact query and corresponding page, and the same country/device filters. For newly added pages, record discovery and index status separately from impressions and clicks. Prioritize Sports average position and CTR: the reported 0.64% CTR alone does not identify whether the cause is rank, intent or the snippet. Avoid changing titles daily while collecting evidence. Inspect the homepage and topic URLs individually rather than infer indexing from public HTTP success.

Google can choose a different search-result title and must recrawl/reprocess changes. This release does not prove that the 79 previously reported Crawled — currently not indexed URLs are now indexed. No new GSC request was made. Existing sitemap URLs now serve 147 canonical content pages and 35 focused-guide URLs.
